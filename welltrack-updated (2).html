🗓️ Local data retention

WellTrack stores your records only in this browser. There is no GitHub or cloud backup. Daily records older than 30 days are automatically deleted when you open or use the app. If you do not update your tracker data for 30 days, the current tracker snapshot is cleared too.

Export anything you want to keep before it expires. Local data can also be lost if you clear browser site data, change browsers, or use another device.
